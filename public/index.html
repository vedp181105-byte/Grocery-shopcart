About Indian Grocery Store

Your trusted premium grocery destination since 2010

Our Story

Indian Grocery Store was born in 2010 from one simple belief: every family deserves access to genuinely fresh, high-quality groceries at honest prices. What started as a small family store in Surat has grown into a beloved neighbourhood supermarket serving over 10,000 families daily.

We work directly with 50+ certified organic farms, cutting out the middlemen to bring produce from field to your doorstep within 24 hours — preserving every vitamin, every flavour, every nutrient.

Our Promise

If it's not fresh enough for our own families, it won't be on your shelf. Every product is quality-checked, every delivery is tracked, and every customer is valued.
